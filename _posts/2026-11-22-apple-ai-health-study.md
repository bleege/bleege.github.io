_Advancing understanding of how people actually use conversational AI health tools._
<!--excerpt.end-->

Last Sunday at the [HLTH conference](https://hlth.com/) in Las Vegas, [Apple announced the Apple Health AI Usage Study](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/11/apple-launches-research-study-to-explore-the-use-of-ai-for-health/), a research study and companion iPhone app built in collaboration with [OpenEvidence](https://www.openevidence.com). The study is open to up to 200,000 participants in the U.S. and explores what makes people trust a health AI answer, how well they understand it, and what they do next.

Getting here took a team of iOS engineers, designers, researchers, clinicians, product managers, and privacy and legal partners at Apple, working alongside OpenEvidence, whose clinical AI platform draws on sources like [The New England Journal of Medicine](https://www.nejm.org/), [JAMA](https://jamanetwork.com) and [Nature](https://www.nature.com).

I worked on the app as an iOS engineer at Apple. It built on my earlier health work at Apple on [LumiHealth with Singapore's Health Promotion Board](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2020/09/singapore-and-apple-partner-on-national-health-initiative-using-apple-watch/).  The app we delivered includes:

* In-app study information and enrollment, so people understand the study before they choose to join
* A conversational experience for everyday health questions, answered in approachable language
* Linked, peer-reviewed clinical citations behind every response
* Visit prep summaries to bring to an appointment with your own doctor
* Optional HealthKit sharing (resting heart rate, activity, sleep, lab results) and in-app surveys
* Privacy and safety guardrails, including a commitment that study data isn't used to train AI models 🩺

I'm very proud of what we delivered, and grateful to have worked on something that puts trust and clinical accuracy first. I'm looking forward to seeing what the study teaches all of us about how people use AI for their health.

If you're in the U.S. with an iPhone 15 Pro or later, you can find the [Apple Health AI Study app on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/apple-health-ai-study-app/id6800060365).
